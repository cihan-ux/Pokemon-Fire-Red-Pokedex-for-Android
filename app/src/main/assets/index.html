Strategy walkthrough

Finish the National Dex.

A Generation 3–accurate roadmap for your FireRed + LeafGreen + Emerald collection. No modern Pokémon mechanics.

Your actual finish line: 380 Pokémon

The National Diploma in FireRed/LeafGreen excludes Mew, Lugia, Ho-Oh, Celebi, Jirachi, and Deoxys. All 386 is a separate, bigger personal challenge.

380 required
STEP 01 / COMPLETE

✓ Kanto + LeafGreen Pokémon

You've said your Kanto collection and LeafGreen exclusives are finished. The tracker starts with Kanto marked owned except event-only Mew.

STEP 02 / YOUR NEXT TARGET

Hunt Suicune

Your original starter was Charmander, so Suicune is your FireRed roaming beast after the Ruby/Sapphire quest. Repel with a Lv. 49 lead and bring Mean Look or another trapping move.

STEP 03 / JOHTO

Catch the Johto species

Check the Sevii Islands and Emerald's expanded Safari Zone after the Hall of Fame. Get Johto starters from Emerald after completing its Hoenn Dex, or transfer from an eligible game.

STEP 04 / HOENN

Transfer from Emerald

Capture Hoenn Pokémon in Emerald, evolve them, and trade to FireRed after unlocking the Network Machine. Some version-exclusive Pokémon still need Ruby or Sapphire.

STEP 05 / TRADES

Finish trade evolutions

Alakazam, Gengar, Machamp, Golem, Steelix, Scizor, Kingdra, Porygon2, Slowking, Politoed, Huntail, and Gorebyss need trades (some with held items).

STEP 06 / LEGENDARIES

Complete Hoenn legends

Collect Regirock, Regice, Registeel, Latias, Latios, Kyogre, Groudon, and Rayquaza. Some depend on version, roaming choices, or special distribution.

⚠ Generation 3 trading matters. FireRed/LeafGreen must have Celio’s Ruby and Sapphire delivered to allow compatible Hoenn-region trading. Emerald is a separate game: Pokémon HOME and later-generation games cannot directly trade back into these GBA saves. GameCube transfers also require the relevant equipment or compatible save/emulation setup.
