DexQuest is an unofficial, open-source Pokédex tracker for Generation III games. Track up to 386 Pokémon, filter by region or collection status, consult an acquisition guide, and back up or restore your progress as JSON. Core tracking works offline; optional character artwork and live encounter data require an internet connection. The app does not require Google Play Services or an account.
