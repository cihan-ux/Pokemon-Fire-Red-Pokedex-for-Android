About the Pokédex

Includes 1,025 National Dex species from Generations 1–9. This is a generic species catalog, not a verified list of Pokémon already released or shiny-eligible in GO. Region variants, costumes, forms and individual Pokémon are not separately tracked in v1.0.

Independent fan project, not endorsed by Niantic, Scopely or The Pokémon Company. Optional Pokémon artwork requires internet; tracking, planning and saved data work offline.
